Understanding the Problem Statement

Why automated, AI-driven spatio-temporal groundwater forecasting is vital for India's food security, rural drinking water, and climate resilience.

Traditional Monitoring Limitations

Historically, groundwater levels across India were manually tape-measured only 4 times a year. This creates massive temporal blind spots, delayed reporting by 6-12 months, and zero early-warning capabilities before borewells dry up.

  • Sparse quarterly manual readings
  • Zero early-warning forecasting
  • High human labor & telemetry gaps

AquaPredict AI Innovation

AquaPredict builds an automated multi-source hydrological pipeline. By ingesting 15-minute DWLR sensor telemetry, NASA GRACE satellite gravity field anomalies, Sentinel-2 soil moisture indices, and IMD gridded rainfall, our deep learning engine forecasts aquifer depths up to 3 years in advance.

  • Real-time continuous DWLR sync
  • Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT) AI
  • 1 to 36-month predictive horizons

Governance & Field Impact

Translates raw complex hydrogeological data into actionable policy recommendations: automated SMS alerts to farmers for precision crop water-budgeting, optimal sites for artificial recharge shafts (Atal Bhujal Yojana), and dynamic extraction licensing for CGWA authorities.

  • Atal Bhujal Yojana synergy
  • Crop diversification advisories
  • Groundwater budget compliance

Technical Design & Data Engineering

System Architecture & ML Pipeline

End-to-end multi-modal data fusion pipeline combining remote sensing, edge IoT, deep learning time-series models, and microservices.

01

Multi-Source Ingestion

Continuous ingestion from 4 primary channels:

  • • CGWB DWLR Telemetry (15-min)
  • • NASA GRACE Gravity Anomalies
  • • Sentinel-2 Surface Moisture
  • • IMD 0.25° Gridded Rainfall
02

Spatial Interpolation & ETL

Data cleaning, noise reduction & imputation:

  • • Extended Kalman Filtering
  • • Universal Kriging Interpolation
  • • Soil Moisture Index (NDVI/NDWI)
  • • Seasonal De-trending
03

Hybrid ML Ensemble

Deep learning & gradient-boosted forecasting:

  • • Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT)
  • • Multi-Layer Bi-LSTM with Attention
  • • XGBoost Spatial Regressor
  • • Monte Carlo Uncertainty Bounds
04

Decision Support Systems

Actionable delivery to stakeholders:
